      CORE PROSPECTS

      Property / Freight Carriers

      Applicants for Motor Carrier of Property (Except Household Goods). These are for-hire commercial trucking carriers transporting general freight and represent the highest-intent targets for independent freight dispatchers.

      OPTIONAL SHORTLIST

      Household Goods Movers

      Applicants for Motor Carrier of Household Goods. They transport personal belongings and furniture. You can include or exclude movers from the Relevant tab at any time using the filter toggle.

      SPECIALTY FREIGHT

      Enterprise Property Carriers

      FMCSA designates Enterprise carriers as international cargo operators. They receive an automated Operating Scope Check flag to remind dispatch teams to verify international or cross-border lanes.

      CROSS-BORDER

      Mexico-Domiciled Carriers

      Carriers domiciled in Mexico operating in US commercial zones or beyond. They carry a Cross-Border Scope Verification flag. Mexico-domiciled private carriers are excluded unless holding a for-hire carrier authority.

      EXCLUDED ENTITIES

      Brokers & Forwarders

      Freight brokers and freight forwarders arrange transportation but do not operate trucks. Passenger operators transport people. These are strictly excluded to protect dispatchers from dialing intermediaries.

      VERIFICATION PROTOCOL

      SAFER Authority Check

      The FMCSA Register is an application notice, not granted operating authority. Before dispatching or booking freight, always verify the active certificate and insurance filings on SAFER.
