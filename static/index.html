      FIELD MANUAL FMCSA Dispatch Intelligence & Regulatory Guide

      FMCSA Dispatch Prospecting & Regulatory Field Manual

      A practical operational reference for identifying high-intent carrier applicants, performing rapid 4-point SAFER compliance checks, and executing high-conversion outbound dispatch outreach.

      CORE TARGET · RELEVANT

      For-Hire Property / Freight Carriers

      Applicants for Motor Carrier of Property (Except Household Goods). These are for-hire commercial trucking companies operating dry van, reefer, and flatbed freight. Because they are in the active phase of launching operations and obtaining authority, they represent the single highest-intent client segment for independent freight dispatchers.

      Tactical Outreach Window: Contact carriers within 24–48 hours of register publication. New entrants are eager to secure freight partners before competing dispatchers flood them.
      SPECIALIZED NICHE · TOGGLEABLE

      Household Goods (HHG) Movers

      Applicants for Motor Carrier of Household Goods. They transport personal residential items, commercial office furniture, and residential relocations. In R2D, you can include or exclude movers from your Relevant prospects list at any time using the checkbox filter.

      Niche Opportunity: Ideal target if your dispatch agency specializes in box trucks, straight trucks, or local distribution logistics rather than standard 53ft OTR dry van loads.
      COMMERCIAL FREIGHT

      Enterprise Property Carriers

      Classified by FMCSA as enterprise commercial operators, frequently managing specialized international, cross-state, or multi-jurisdiction freight corridors. R2D automatically flags them with an Operating Scope Check reminder to verify regional and lane requirements.

      Fleet Verification: Confirm operating lanes, equipment certifications, and tractor counts on SAFER prior to booking high-volume corporate freight.
      CROSS-BORDER FREIGHT

      Mexico-Domiciled Motor Carriers

      Carriers domiciled in Mexico authorized to operate in US border commercial zones or designated long-haul corridors. Marked in R2D with Cross-Border Verification. Private Mexican carriers are quarantined unless holding certified for-hire authority.

      Border Compliance Check: Verify active CABT safety inspection decals and US commercial zone operating permits before arranging interchange freight.
      AUTOMATICALLY EXCLUDED

      Freight Brokers & Forwarders

      Freight brokers and freight forwarders arrange and intermediate transportation but do not own or operate commercial motor vehicles. Passenger operators transport people. R2D automatically quarantines these entities in the Excluded view to protect you from dialer fatigue.

      Zero Waste Outbound: Dispatchers represent motor carriers, not brokers. Keeping brokers quarantined ensures 100% of your calling list consists of asset-based carriers.
      COMPLIANCE PROTOCOL

      The SAFER 4-Point Authority Check

      The FMCSA Register is an application notice, not granted operating authority. Carriers must submit insurance and agent filings before authority is activated. Run this rapid 4-point check via the 1-Click SAFER link:

      • 1. Operating Status: Check that status reads AUTHORIZED FOR Property.
      • 2. Insurance Filings: Verify active BMC-91X ($750k or $1M BIPD).
      • 3. BOC-3 Process Agent: Confirm active process agent filing.
      • 4. Safety Rating: Verify rating is None (new entrant) or Satisfactory.
      1-Click Efficiency: Click the SAFER link in the table next to any USDOT to open the official company snapshot in a single click.
      DISPATCH WORKFLOW

      High-Cadence Morning Outreach Routine

      Top dispatch agencies maintain a disciplined 3-step morning workflow to connect with carriers before their competition wakes up:

      1. 1. Ingest Daily PDF: Drop the newly published daily FMCSA bulletin into the Upload tab (takes ~15 seconds).
      2. 2. State Filter: Click your priority dispatch states (e.g. TX, CA, GA, IL) in the state chips bar to filter nearby targets.
      3. 3. Dial Owner-Operators: Use 1-Click Call or Phone Copy to pitch new carriers directly while reviewing their SAFER snapshot.
      Speed Advantage: Calling within the first 2 hours of publication yields a 3x higher conversation rate than dialing stale lists.
      LEAD INTEL

      What the "2x" / "3x" Occurrence Badges Mean

      When an applicant files in multiple daily registers (reapplication, authority scope change, or corrected filing), R2D detects the duplicate USDOT and flags it with an occurrence badge (e.g. 2x Filed). R2D presents their latest filed contact details while retaining full source records in their dossier.

      High Intent Signal: Carriers with 2+ filings are actively working through regulatory requirements and are exceptionally eager to start hauling freight.
